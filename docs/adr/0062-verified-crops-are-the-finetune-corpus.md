# ADR-0062: Verified crops are the extraction fine-tuning corpus

**Status:** Accepted
**Accepted:** 2026-08-17
**Date:** 2026-08-17
**Supersedes:** none
**Superseded by:** none

## Context

Fine-tuning the extraction model on local receipts would improve accuracy on exactly the
distribution that matters: Turkish thermal print, local POS layouts, real photographic conditions.

The obvious source of training data, a scraped product catalog, is the wrong one. It contains product
names but no images and no supervision, so it cannot train a vision model at all.

The right data is being produced anyway, as a byproduct of review.

## Decision

Every confirmed T2 review is an (image region, confirmed text) pair on a real local
receipt in real conditions.

These accumulate as a byproduct of the review loop and constitute the correct corpus for a domain
fine-tune, when volume justifies one.

They are held under the same subject keys as their originals, so an erased contributor's pairs are
shredded with everything else, and the corpus shrinks accordingly (ADR-0073).

The confirmed text labels survive severed even when the image is destroyed, which retains their value
for lexicon purposes though not for vision training.

## Consequences

A fine-tuning corpus builds itself from work already being done, at no additional
collection cost.

The corpus is biased toward hard cases, since T2 tasks are generated by routing signals rather than
sampled uniformly (ADR-0058). That is useful for training and must be understood rather than assumed
away.

Erasure shrinks it, which is stated rather than discovered.

Fine-tuning would produce a model whose version must be recorded like any other, and a reprocessing
decision follows (ADR-0082).

## Alternatives considered

**Fine-tune on scraped catalog data.** Rejected. No images, no supervision, wrong
modality entirely.

**Hand-label a training set.** Rejected. Expensive, and the review loop produces the same thing for
free.

**Never fine-tune.** Not rejected. It remains optional, and the corpus accrues whether or not it is
used.

## Revisit trigger

Corpus volume and measured extraction error rate together justify the cost of a
fine-tuning run.
